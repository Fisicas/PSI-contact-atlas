Processing, downloads and limitations

Map values are retained from the prepared source crop; no additional map normalization, smoothing, sharpening, fitting or coordinate refinement is performed by this viewer. A model-centered carve is a display aid, not independent support. Turning it off shows the retained map box, not the whole complex. The browser triangulation and camera differ from the fixed PyMOL still.
