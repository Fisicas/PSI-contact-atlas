# Comparison scope

This atlas contains selected, source-bound PSI donor and quinone environments. It is separate from the structural census and is not an exhaustive inventory of PSI structures, species, antenna architecture or possible contacts.

The 14 baseline entries retain their curated/template-derived scene selections. The eight additions (9SE6, 9LUT, 8WEY, 7DKZ, 9UH3, 9HYU, 9KZ9, 8XLS) also received a prospective diagnostic-neighborhood supplement: core-subunit N/O atoms within 3.5 Å of the declared diagnostic pigment oxygens, with their residue heavy atoms. The 9HYU core includes both PsaA fragments A/a and PsaB B. Water rules are unchanged.

This supplement was not retroactively applied to the 14 baseline entries. Therefore scene pair counts are not equivalent, exhaustive contact inventories and must not be used as a cross-species count comparison. A blank/missing comparison cell can mean that the homologous atom, residue, component or deposited water is absent/unavailable under the stated rule; it does not establish biological absence. Read the missing-reason field.

Comparison columns evaluate declared homologous pairs against the deposited models. Interpret the same pair with its native identities, preparation, method/resolution, component identity, validation caveats and source selection. Coordinate precision, displayed decimal places and nominal resolution do not supply a distance uncertainty. Template/model ancestry and related reconstructions limit independence; deposited protomers are not biological replicates.

No new scientific uniform re-extraction across the entire baseline is included in this release candidate. Such a change would require a separate specification and review. Source-specific geometry concerns are recorded in [the scientific review](SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW.md) and [per-cofactor validation context](../downloads/validation/cofactor_geometry.csv).
