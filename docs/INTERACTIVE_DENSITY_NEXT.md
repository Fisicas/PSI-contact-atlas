# Interactive density — integrated development feature

*Historical review record. For v1.1.0 publication and citation information, see [release notes](RELEASE_NOTES_1.1.0.md) and the [citation guide](CITATION.md).*

The earlier one-scene pilot has been extended to all 39 protomers with available experimental data (156 scenes). All 208 mesh thumbnails now open their exact interactive scene and contour, with direct static PNG fallbacks. The published atlas remains unchanged pending review.

See [current methods, controls, verification and limitations](INTERACTIVE_DENSITY.md) and [release notes](RELEASE_NOTES_1.1.0.md). The earlier separate pilot is preserved locally as historical development evidence; its one-scene checks are not substituted for the new all-scene checks.
